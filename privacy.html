Privacy Policy — Solist Game

Last updated: November 21, 2026. Developer: M.Sadr (solist.ir).

What we collect

Nothing. Solist Game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. We do not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your best scores are saved in your device’s local storage, and the game files are cached so it works offline. This data never leaves your device and you can erase it by clearing the app’s data.

Permissions

The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Links

The menu links to solist.ir and Instagram (mohammad.sadr.music). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: solist.ir or Instagram mohammad.sadr.music.
